# Pitch notes — decisions and open questions

## How the concept was put together

- **Structure:** a proven edible-brand site architecture: a gummies hub, one page per product line, one page per
  flavor, store finder, story, help-center FAQ, lab results, press, blog, contact, careers and legal pages.
- **Content:** written for Hashables only. Facts come from hashables.com, the new homepage draft and the brand's
  direction (flavor first, "Tastes like paradise", fresh pressed, solventless, uplifting chill, a retro beachy surfy
  look). Nothing was copied from another brand's site.
- **Awards** are limited to wins we could verify in public sources (High Times 2022 and 2023, Harvest Cup 2023), each
  linked to its source, plus the badges in Hashables' own art.
- **Design** keeps the brand: the brush wordmark, the pink-palms and swirl art, the kit colours (#FFF200, #FF7CA5,
  #3FACC9, #A1C7EE, #FFD85D, #011935) and Aldrich, then adds glass, a synthwave sunset and depth.

## Recommendations

- **Store finder:** the concept keeps the live Lit Alerts map and wraps it in a phone-first guide plus a "tell us where
  to call" request form. The next step is a native finder fed by a daily inventory check, so a visitor can search by
  town or location and only see shops that carry Hashables today.
- **Photography:** the HSH26 packaging tiles are 680 px masters, sharp at card and product-page size. Larger tile
  exports and the remaining six bag renders would let the packaging run bigger on large screens.
- **Forms:** the request, contact and COA forms open the visitor's email app (no backend). Connect them to Nova's CRM
  or helpdesk for launch.
- **Brand fonts:** swap in the licensed brand script face for the stand-in once files are supplied.
